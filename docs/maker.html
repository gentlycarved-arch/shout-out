Make your maker card

For the people who make the shaders, skills and tools.

Your maker card says who you are and how you’d like to be credited, in your own words. When someone credits your work with Shoutout, their agent reads your card first, so your name, your bio and your links are the ones that show. It also puts you in Discover, unless you’d rather not be listed.

Where to put it

  

    
On your own site
Upload it as maker.json at the top of your site, or at /.well-known/maker.json. Or put it anywhere and add <link rel="maker" href="/me.json"> to your home page.

    
No site of your own
Make a public GitHub Gist named maker.json and use its “Raw” link.

  


  
Get listed

  
Once your card is up, add yourself to Discover with its address. It’s checked and added the same day.

  
Rather not be found at all? Untick “List me” above and keep the card up, or ask to be left out with the link sites use for you. Your work is then left out of Discover and of makers’ search. Sites’ own Shoutout buttons are theirs and don’t change.
